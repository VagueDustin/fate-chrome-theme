A deep navy and metallic gold theme for Chrome, drawn from the VagueDustin Enterprises design language.

Every surface is covered — window frame, tab strip, toolbar, omnibox, bookmarks bar and the new tab page. Gold is reserved for the controls you actually press: back, forward, reload, menu. Everything else stays quiet, so the browser reads as still until you reach for it.

The new tab page carries a layered navy depth wash, a scattered starfield, four-point sparkles, and a crescent moon struck in antique gold foil.

WHICH BUILD IS THIS

Gilded Fate is the ceremonial build — brighter gold, ornamental corner brackets, and a fine film grain over the whole surface. If you want something more restrained to sit behind a full working day, install FATE — Gold & Navy instead. Same palette, less ornament.

WHAT IT DOESN'T DO

No permissions. No background code. No network access. No analytics, no tracking, and no data collected of any kind. It is a theme and nothing else.

Navy and gold, inscribed rather than printed, lit from somewhere just off the page.
